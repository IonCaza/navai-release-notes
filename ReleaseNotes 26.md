# Release Notes 1.27

**Release Version:** 2026 version 1.27

This communication is for notifying users of NAV AI about the enhancements made to the application.

---

## Features Released

### AI Assistant: now available inside the flow builder

The AI assistant is now accessible directly inside the flow builder. The chat panel stays on top of builder pop-ups, so you can ask for help right where you are working and close the panel when you are done.

---

### Contact support [NEW]

If you need help, you can now open your profile menu and select Support. You will be directed to a page with contact details (email and phone number) to get in touch with our global technical support team and with guidance on what to include so your issue gets resolved faster. It's monitored 24/7.

---

## Other Improvements

- **Error messages:** error messages are now shown in plain language across the platform.
- **Cancel document uploads:** you can now cancel an upload even after processing has started.
- **Conversation Starter Prompts** are now called Suggested Prompts. And they stay visible throughout a whole conversation.
