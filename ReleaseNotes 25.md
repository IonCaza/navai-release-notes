# Release Notes 1.25 & 1.26
## Flows: more routing options

- **More branches per node**  
  Conditional Handoffs were previously limited to two branches. You can now add as many condition branches as you need in a single node — more routing flexibility, fewer extra nodes cluttering your flow.
- **See exactly how agents pass work to each other**  
  Connection lines between agents now show a labelled badge with the handoff type. Hover over any line to delete it directly.
- **Consistent editor across all modes**  
  Edit, View, and Preview now behave the same way. What you configure is what you see, regardless of which mode you are in.
- **Clearer error messages**  
  If something is missing or incomplete, you will now see a plain-language message telling you exactly what to fix — before you try to save.

## AI Assistant: smarter and more context-aware

- The assistant now understands what you are working on, not just which page you are on. Suggestions are based on your current task, making guidance more relevant in the moment.
- You can also now make changes just by telling the assistant what you need. For example: "remove this flow I no longer use". The assistant will show you a clear plan of what it is about to do. Nothing changes until you confirm.

## Two menu items have been renamed

- **Workflows are now Flows**
- **Worksheets are now Notebooks**, with the ability to make personal contributions directly within a Notebook

## Data Marketplace: clearer structure

- The hierarchy is now:  *Data Marketplace > Datasets (collections of data) > Data sources (the files within each dataset)*.
  Same content, clearer labels.

## For admins only: legal notices

- You can now configure the legal notice users see at login — including adding organisation-specific content alongside the standard Terms and Conditions.
- You can also require users to re-accept at every login via the re-acknowledgment setting.

## Note for all users

- You will see a Terms and Conditions prompt on your first login. You need to accept it to access the platform.
